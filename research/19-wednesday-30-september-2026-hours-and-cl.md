# Wednesday 30 September 2026 hours-and-closures audit, berth question, and 2026 openings (Hassan Bey, Pervola, seaside walkway) for the Scarlet Lady call at Rhodes

## The day sheet: what is actually open on Wednesday 30 September 2026

**The clock that matters is not the Palace's closing time.** The ship's all-aboard is 17:30, so whether the Palace shuts at 19:00 or 20:00 is moot. The Ministry's own 2026 pages (odysseus.culture.gr, "Summer: from 1 April 2026") give the Palace of the Grand Master **daily 08:00–20:00**, but every *exhibition inside it closes at 17:00*, the walls walk is a **12:00–15:00 weekday window**, and the small Ministry collections shut at **15:30**. Build the day around those inner deadlines, not the outer one.

**The Palace's Wednesday rota is contradictory on the Ministry's own site.** Page obj_id 6301 (the museum entry, which also carries the "from 1 November 2025" winter rota and a maintenance note, so it looks like the newer edit) says: *"Ancient Rhodes – 2,400 Years" open Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday 09:00–17:00; "Rhodes from the 4th c. AD until its conquest by the Turks (1522)" open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 09:00–17:00; "From the Knights of St John to the Knights of Malta" daily 09:00–17:00; Gardens of the Grand Master's Palace daily 09:00–17:00; the lower level of the RHODES 2400 YEARS exhibition is closed for maintenance.* Page obj_id 5028 (the monument entry) says the opposite pairing: 2,400 Years on Tue/Thu/Sat and the Byzantine exhibition on Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun. Either way, on a Wednesday exactly **one** of the two big exhibitions is open 09:00–17:00 plus the Knights-of-Malta room and the gardens; the report that the Byzantine exhibition is Wednesday-closed matches the newer page. Ticket: the Ministry lists **€20 full**, "valid for Archaeological Museum, Decorative Arts Collection, Our Lady of the Castle (Panagia tou Kastrou), Medieval Town of Rhodes, Palace" – a jump from earlier years, so confirm at the desk. The **€11 million** restoration (Region of South Aegean funding to the Ministry, announced 27 August 2026) is a funding agreement only; no works, wing closures or start date have been announced.

**Archaeological Museum (Hospital of the Knights).** Daily 08:00–20:00, €10, page last updated 28/07/2026. The main galleries, the Aphrodite of Rhodes and the courtyard are unaffected.

## The three new 2026 venues

**Hassan Bey Mansion, Argyrokastrou Square** (36.44574, 28.22765). Delivered 10–11 June 2026 (€2M; structural reinforcement, stonework, wooden staircase, roof, new services). In 2022 HPPC announced it would be a "Centre for Presenting the History of the City of Rhodes through the Years"; the Ministry's June 2026 text calls it "a new cultural hub of the Medieval City, ideal for hosting various cultural activities." No opening hours, fee or September programme have been published. Score it as a facade-and-doorway stop: the tall blind ground-floor wall and the row of first-floor windows of an early-19th-century Ottoman konak, "in front of the tourist port".

**Pervola open-air museum** (shipyards/tetrapylon at 36.44576, 28.22607). Inaugurated 13 June 2026 by the Prime Minister, formally opened 14 June by Minister Mendoni: about 20 stremmata that "starts from the Palace of the Grand Master", sealed for 75 years as a depot. Mendoni: *"we have Hellenistic shipyards, the Roman Cardo and Decumanus, medieval fortifications, Ottoman buildings, up to more recent times."* More than 4,000 stone artefacts identified, 8,500 architectural pieces recorded, 18,000 objects moved; a 700-piece outdoor sculpture gallery and the replanted Governor's gardens. Cost €4.5M. Hours and fee were not published; the Palace listing's "Gardens daily 09:00–17:00" is the best proxy and a Palace ticket may be required. Gate location unverified – approach from the Palace/Kleovoulou Square side.

**Seaside fortifications walkway** (€1.2M, delivered 10 June 2026): Apostle Paul bastion and Naillac-mole fortifications, the Byzantine sea-gate complex, the spiral stair at the new sea gate, and the upper level of St Catherine's Gate, with "an accessible corridor along the walls" and lighting. Ends: St Paul's Gate/bastion (36.44635, 28.22830) and St Catherine's Gate (36.44383, 28.23211), which is two minutes from the cruise mole. This gives the direct sea-wall route the tour wanted: quay → St Catherine's Gate → along the harbour wall past the Marine Gate → St Paul's Gate → Kolona waterfront → Liberty Gate → Mandraki, about 15 minutes. No hours are published; it was framed as public space "returned to society".
