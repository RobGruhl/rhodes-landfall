# Port and day logistics for Scarlet Lady in Rhodes, Wednesday 30 September 2026 (berths, gates, walking times, taxis, weather/sun, museum hours and tickets)

## The shape of the day

Cruisetimetables' port listing for Rhodes on Wednesday 30 September 2026 shows exactly two ships: **Scarlet Lady (Virgin Voyages), 09:00–18:00**, on the "7 Night Greek Isles & Mykonos Overnight" voyage out of Piraeus (voyage ID SC2609277NGMK, 27 Sep–4 Oct: Bodrum 29 Sep 08:00–20:00, Rhodes 30 Sep, Mykonos overnight 1–2 Oct, Santorini 3 Oct), and **Celestyal Discovery, 07:00–18:00**. Virgin's own voyage-planner page confirms the port sequence "Athens (Piraeus), Bodrum, Rhodes, Mykonos, Mykonos, Santorini & Athens" but does not print times, so the 09:00–18:00 window comes from the aggregator, not from Virgin directly. Sunrise-sunset.org gives **sunrise 07:00, sunset 18:54, civil dusk 19:18 (EEST, UTC+3)** for that date. The scriptwriter's gift here: the ship sails at 18:00, fifty-four minutes before sunset. The birthday sunset happens from the top deck with the walls, the Fort of St Nicholas and the three windmills of Mandraki sliding astern, and nobody has to hurry back for it.

Weather is the kindest of the year. HNMS normals (1995–2025, Rhodes airport, as tabulated on Wikipedia) give September a mean high of 28.2 °C, mean low 22.9 °C, only 7.9 mm of rain and 307 sunshine hours; October drops to 24.6/19.3 °C and 42 mm. Sea temperature is 26.7 °C in September. Expect roughly 26–28 °C by early afternoon, a north-westerly breeze on the Mandraki side, and warm water for a Kallithea swim.

## Where the ship ties up, and the walk in

Rhodes has three basins on the town's east flank. Cruise ships normally use the **Tourist Port (Emporio / commercial harbour)** on **Akti Sachtouri**, a few hundred metres from the walls, with the terminal building at 36.44511, 28.23313 and the marked "Taxi Station Tourist Port" rank at 36.44449, 28.23235; the rhodesport.gr portal describes the taxi "main rank at port entrance", and whatsinport calls the cruise berth the "Tourist Harbor (Cruise terminal)… a very short stroll (5 min.) away from the beautiful old town". The larger **Akandia** basin (commercial harbour node 36.44243, 28.23690) lies immediately south and is used as an overflow/large-ship berth; cruisetimetables' port blurb even says ships dock at "Akantia Port… a short walk south from the pier leads to the historic walled Old Town". With Scarlet Lady at 278 m and Celestyal Discovery alongside the same morning, the berth assignment is genuinely uncertain, so the audio tour should trigger from either quay. Note also that the municipal waterfront project "from Akandia port to the new marina" (Dimokratiki, 21 May 2025) was extended to 21 August 2025 after repeated delays and complaints that cruise passengers were being decanted "directly into an active construction zone"; whether any works persist into autumn 2026 is unverified.

From the Tourist Port terminal the geometry is compact (straight-line distances, add ~30% for real walking): **St Catherine's Gate** 170 m (3 min), **Gate of the Virgin (Panagia)** 300 m (5 min), **Marine Gate** 450 m (7–8 min along the quay), **Palace of the Grand Master** 800 m (13–15 min). From Akandia add roughly 400–500 m: Gate of the Virgin ~10–12 min, Marine Gate ~13–15 min. The natural first entrance for a cruise passenger is the **Gate of the Virgin** (36.44323, 28.23072), a wide breach the Greek administration opened in 1955 for vehicles, which drops you straight into the Jewish quarter (La Juderia) and, 120 m later, at **Kahal Shalom** (36.44215, 28.23039). The more atmospheric entrance is **St Catherine's Gate** (36.44386, 28.23211), the "Windmills Gate" that once joined the merchants' town to the harbour grain mills; and the grand one is the **Marine Gate** (36.44404, 28.22823), the harbour's principal entrance, its upper north tower destroyed by Allied bombing and rebuilt in 1951. Wikipedia's circuit runs: d'Amboise (below the Palace, triple defensive belt) → St Athanasios (1441–42, the gate through which tradition says Suleiman's troops entered in 1522, walled up until the Italians reopened it in 1922) → St John / Kokkini Porta ("Red Gate", said to be dyed with the blood of the 1522 siege) → Akandia (Italian, 1935) → St Catherine → Virgin (1955) → Marine → Arnaldo (access to the Hospital) → Arsenal (14th c., Heredia's arms) → St Paul → Liberty (Italian, 1924). Coordinates for all of them are in the places list.

## Tickets and hours that actually apply to that Wednesday

Greek state sites run the **summer timetable from 1 April to 31 October**; the Ministry's Odysseus pages for both the Palace and the Archaeological Museum state "Winter: From 1 November 2025, Wednesday to Monday 08:30–15:30, Tuesday closed" and "Summer: From 1 April 2026, Daily 08:00–20:00". So 30 September is comfortably inside the long-hours regime, and being a Wednesday it avoids the winter Tuesday closure anyway. The free "European Heritage Days" weekend is the *last weekend of September* — 26–27 September 2026 — so no free entry on the 30th.

- **Palace of the Grand Master** (Kleovoulou Sq.): daily 08:00–20:00. The Odysseus page now lists **Full €20**, with the "special ticket package" also €20 covering the Archaeological Museum, Decorative Arts Collection and Panagia tou Kastrou. Inside, the exhibition "Ancient Rhodes – 2,400 Years" opens **Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday 09:00–17:00** (so it is open on the 30th), whereas "Rhodes from the 4th c. AD until 1522" opens Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri only (closed that Wednesday); "From the Knights of St John to the Knights of Malta" and the gardens are daily 09:00–17:00; the lower level of the 2,400 Years exhibition is closed for maintenance. Access note from the Ministry: "By car to the Amboise Gate or the Liberty Gate and then on foot."
- **Archaeological Museum (Hospital of the Knights)**: daily 08:00–20:00; Odysseus lists **Full €10** and (inconsistently) a €10 package. The Prehistoric Collection opens Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun 09:00–17:00 (open on the 30th); the Epigraphic Collection and Villaragut Mansion open Tue/Thu/Sat only (closed on the 30th).
- **Panagia tou Kastrou (Byzantine Museum)**: "Summer from April 2026: Wednesday to Monday 08:30–15:30, Tuesday closed"; ticket €5 on its page. Its practical openness is patchy — treat as "probably open until 15:30".
- **Decorative Arts Collection** (the Arsenal on Argyrokastrou Sq.): the Odysseus page prints €5 and an empty "Open" field — assume closed unless a door is visibly open.
- **Modern Greek Art Museum (Nestoridion Melathron, Gavriil Charitou Sq.)**: Tue–Sat 10:00–21:00, last entry 20:30, closed Sun/Mon; €6 / €3 reduced, valid for all venues for a week; the Old-Town "Center of Modern Art (Old Syssitio)" runs Tue–Sat 10:00–17:00 through 31 October.
- **Acropolis of Rhodes / Monte Smith**: "Open-air site. Permanently open." Free. About 1.8 km walk (25 min, uphill) from the Palace, or a short metered taxi.
- **Kallithea Springs**: high season 1 May–31 Oct, **08:00–20:00, €5** (official site kallitheasprings.com); a 7.4 km taxi hop south.
- **Kahal Shalom & Jewish Museum**: **10:00–15:00, 1 April–31 October, closed Saturdays and state holidays, €6** (under-12s free); the whole complex shuts from 1 November.
- **Roloi Clock Tower** (36.44423, 28.22376): privately run viewpoint-with-café at the highest point of the Old Town; hours and the widely reported "€5 including a drink" could not be verified from any reachable 2025–26 source.

## Taxis and getting further out

The only published fixed-fare list found is rhodesguide.com's 2022 table from Rhodes Town: Kallithea Springs €14, Ladiko/Anthony Quinn Bay €22, Faliraki €27, airport €25, Lindos €62, plus a €1.08 port surcharge and doubled tariff 00:00–05:00; Wikivoyage's 2019 figures were Kallithea €15, Faliraki €20, in-town €5–6. Expect 2026 numbers to be higher; the union's laminated board at the Tourist Port rank is authoritative. Uber (dispatching licensed taxis) has operated in Rhodes since April 2023. The main town rank is at Mandraki/Rimini Square (36.44722, 28.22625), an 11-minute walk from the terminal; Monte Smith is a short metered ride (roughly 3 km by road). Anthony Quinn Bay is 13.8 km straight-line (about 20 km by road), so budget 30 minutes each way and ask the driver to wait or arrange a return.

## A birthday-shaped timeline

09:00 gangway; 09:10 St Catherine's Gate; 09:15 Kahal Shalom opens at 10:00, so go first to the Marine Gate, Hospital and Street of the Knights while the light is low and the alleys empty; 10:00–11:30 Palace (the 2,400 Years exhibition is open on Wednesdays); 12:00 Roloi for the panorama; lunch in the Old Town; 14:00 taxi to Kallithea for a swim in the Italian rotunda's cove (open to 20:00); 16:30 back at the quay with an hour's margin before the 17:30 all-aboard; 18:00 sailaway; 18:54 sunset from the deck.
