1. 1444 Mamluk siege: 10 August-18 September 1444, 85 ships under Aynal Gecut, landed north of Trianda, concentrated on the west side 'especially the poorly defended gate of Agios Antonios' while a second column raided Mandraki and burned ships; lifted 13 September 'contrary to the orders of their sultan' (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Rhodes_(1444)). kastra.eu adds a failed Mamluk attempt in 1440 and notes the moles of St Nicholas and the Mills were unfortified until 1444, which is why Fort St Nicholas was built (https://www.kastra.eu/castleen.php?kastro=rodoscity).

3. 1306-1310: the Hospitallers' own siege of the Byzantine city, begun summer 1306 with 35 knights and 500 foot; a Byzantine relief of eight galleys in 1307; city surrendered 15 August 1310 (date debated 1308-1310; kastra says 1309) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospitaller_conquest_of_Rhodes).
4. 1481 earthquake: 3 May 1481, Ms 7.1, intensity X, tsunami, c. 30,000 casualties; damaged houses and fortifications a year after the siege (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_earthquakes_in_Greece; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortifications_of_Rhodes).
